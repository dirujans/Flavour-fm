Why Flavour FM exists

Great music starts small. A voice in a bedroom. A beat on a phone. A crowd that doesn't know yet.

Flavour FM is how Wingstop backs that moment for the long run. Five things we do, over and over, for as long as there is new sound to hear.

TRK 01 · SIDE A

Find talent.

In every city there's someone the industry hasn't caught yet. Our creative leads, partners and crews are out in the scenes, listening for the sound before the numbers arrive.

ScoutingOpen submissionsPartner scenes
TRK 02 · SIDE A

Create & connect.

Talent grows in company. We give artists the room, the kit and the time, then put them next to producers, writers and creators they would never otherwise meet.

Studio timeWriting campsCollabs
TRK 03 · SIDE A

Original music.

Everything that comes out of Flavour FM is new. Original tracks, original shows and original sessions, credited to the people who made them and released where their fans listen.

RecordsSessionsShows
TRK 04 · SIDE A

Inspire more.

One artist's breakthrough becomes the next kid's reason to start. We take every story home, with takeovers in their own city and workshops with Youth Music, so the ripple keeps going.

Home-city takeoversThe AcademyLive nights
TRK 05 · SIDE A

Find their flavour.

The point of all of it. A sound that's unmistakably theirs, and the confidence to keep making it. When an artist finds their flavour, everyone around them hears it.

IdentityConfidenceLongevity

Flavour FM Studios · Wingstop HQ, London

Step inside.

A professional three-room studio inside Wingstop HQ, built in partnership with Youth Music. Made for first sessions and finished records.

The room

Three rooms.
One job: get it made.

IN THE ROOM
  • Control roomProduction desk, studio monitors, the screen and the keys
  • Vocal boothThrough the glass. When the light says Mic Live, you're on
  • The loungeWhere sessions start, ideas get played back and people meet

Who it's for

  • Rising artists Writing, recording, finishing
  • Producers Beats, mixes, collabs
  • Creators & hosts Shows, interviews, shorts
  • The Academy Workshops with Youth Music

How a session works

  1. RequestTell us who's coming, what you're making and when suits.
  2. ReviewOur team reads every request and comes back to you by email.
  3. SignStudio agreement and house rules. Filming consent is separate and always your call.
  4. In the roomTurn up, plug in, make the thing.

Request a session → Every session is a request until we confirm it. Under 18? You're welcome here. Sessions need a parent or guardian's consent and a supervising adult in the room.
